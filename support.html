Merchant support

Support has moved to Crabby Support

Pastel Macaroon support requests are handled through the private company support portal at support.crabby.pl.

No GitHub account is required. A successful submission creates a private ticket, returns a ticket ID and sends an acknowledgement to the email address entered in the form. New requests receive an initial response within two business days. Critical defects that block storefront browsing, purchase or checkout handoff are triaged immediately when received.

Send only information needed for diagnosis. Do not provide passwords, customer or payment data, API credentials, Theme Access passwords, private app tokens or unredacted admin screenshots. If store access is needed, support will request time-limited Shopify collaborator access with only the required permissions.

Prepare before opening a request

  • Your name, reply email address and Shopify store URL.
  • The affected product or theme, theme version and page URL.
  • Severity, exact reproduction steps, expected result and actual result.
  • Device, operating system, browser or app version and a clean-copy reproduction result.
  • Up to three relevant JPEG, PNG or WebP screenshots with sensitive information removed.
